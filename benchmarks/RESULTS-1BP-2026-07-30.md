## Summary

**16 models benchmarked successfully** — all achieve **~3.2-4.1 GB/s** single-threaded dequantization throughput on the Zen 5 CPU. The 1BP format provides efficient memory-mappable Q4NX quantization with minimal overhead.

**13 models need loader fixes** — primarily TQ2 ternary (Bonsai family) and certain Q4NX models with edge-case tensor dimensions. The `onebp_loader.cpp` needs to handle:
1. TQ2 ternary dequant path
2. Q4NX tensor index edge cases
3. Vocab_size=0 fallback
