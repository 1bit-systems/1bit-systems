⚠️ DSpark: 572 tok/s was a projection — disproven on hardware

The 5.90 tokens-per-verify figure was measured with DeepSpec on Qwen3-4B (CPU/GPU), not the NPU. The "572 tok/s" number was a projection (base × 5.90×). Measured end-to-end on the real NPU (2026-07-07, Qwen3-0.6B INT8): 0.1–0.2 tok/s at 0% draft acceptance — the draft, trained on HF hidden states, is rejected 100% of the time against the INT8 target. DSpark is a work in progress, not production.
