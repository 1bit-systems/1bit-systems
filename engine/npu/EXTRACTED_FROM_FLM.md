# NPU Utils — Now Official MIT

The `include/npu_utils/` files in this directory were originally reverse-engineered
from FastFlowLM v0.9.46 proprietary binaries.

**As of July 2026:** FastFlowLM was acquired by AMD and moved to
[ROCm/FastFlowLM](https://github.com/ROCm/FastFlowLM) under MIT License.
The NPU-accelerated binary kernels (.xclbin files) are now completely free
for any use, including commercial use.

These files are now synced from the official `ROCm/FastFlowLM` submodule at
`third_party/FastFlowLM/`.

See: https://github.com/ROCm/FastFlowLM
